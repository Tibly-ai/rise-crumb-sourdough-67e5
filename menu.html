Every loaf we shape starts with water, sea salt, and our living wild yeast culture. We stone-mill select heritage grains on-site to lock in complex, rustic flavors.

Below you will find our core offerings along with our rotating weekly specials. Because we bake in small batches and respect the slow fermentation process, everything is available first-come, first-served until the shelves are empty.
