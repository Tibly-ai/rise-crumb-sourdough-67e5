Our Core Philosophy

Slow fermentation reveals the true deep character of grain.

We believe bread should be digestible, flavorful, and honest. Our journey starts with single-origin heritage wheat sourced from regenerative growers. By stone-milling our flour daily, we preserve essential oils and micronutrients lost in industrial milling.

Every loaf undergoes a patient thirty-six hour cold rise, allowing wild yeasts and friendly lactic bacteria to break down complex sugars. The result is a bold, caramelized blistered crust, a custardy interior crumb, and a signature complex flavor profiles that standard bakeries cannot duplicate.

Our Daily Ritual

The 36-Hour Cycle

We honor ancient methodology. There are no chemical additives, dry yeasts, or dough-conditioners used under our roof. Just nature and discipline.

Read Our Full Story
01

The Stone-Grind

We source raw grains direct from organic farms and mill them in our wooden stone mill. This gentle milling prevents heat damage to the precious wheat germ.

02

The Slow Fermentation

Our wild yeast cultures, nurtured since our beginning, digest gluten naturally over thirty-six hours of cold storage, producing complex aromas and depth.

03

The Gentle Shaping

Every single loaf is weighed, tucked, and hand-tensioned into proofing baskets to preserve delicate pockets of air prior to oven contact.

04

The Stone Deck Fire

Loaves slide onto roasting hot stone decks under intense steam. Moisture hits cold dough to prompt deep caramelization, thick crust, and a tender interior.
